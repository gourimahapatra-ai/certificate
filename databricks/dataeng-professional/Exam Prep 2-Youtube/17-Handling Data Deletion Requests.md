# Handling Data Deletion Requests: GDPR & CCPA Compliance

## 1. Understanding GDPR and CCPA Frameworks
- **GDPR (EU)** and **CCPA (California)** define strict rules for handling personal data.
- Both regulations grant individuals the right to request deletion of their personal information.
- Organizations must ensure transparent, secure, and timely processing of such requests.

## 2. Verifying User Identity
- Establish a clear identity‑verification process before deleting data.
- Use secure methods such as:
  - Multi‑factor verification  
  - Email or phone confirmation  
  - Identity documents (when legally appropriate)
- Prevents unauthorized or fraudulent deletion requests.

## 3. Systematic Approach to Data Deletion
- Create a standardized workflow for handling deletion requests:
  - Receive and log the request  
  - Verify identity  
  - Locate all relevant data across systems  
  - Delete or anonymize data  
  - Confirm completion with the requester  
- Ensure deletion covers all storage layers: raw, processed, backups (where legally allowed).

## 4. Documentation and Audit Trail
- Maintain detailed records of:
  - Requests received  
  - Verification steps  
  - Data deleted or anonymized  
  - Dates and responsible personnel  
- Supports compliance audits and internal governance.

## 5. Clear Communication with Users
- Inform users of their rights under GDPR and CCPA.
- Provide transparent explanations of:
  - What data will be deleted  
  - How long the process will take  
  - Any exceptions (e.g., legal retention requirements)
- Confirm completion once the deletion is processed.

## 6. Summary
Handling data deletion requests requires a structured, compliant process aligned with GDPR and CCPA. By verifying identity, documenting actions, and communicating clearly, organizations ensure privacy protection and maintain regulatory compliance.
